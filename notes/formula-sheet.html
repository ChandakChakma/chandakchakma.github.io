The key formulas of the series on one page. The last column links to the post that derives each one.

Idea Formula Section
Policy gradient \(\nabla_\theta J=\mathbb{E}[R\,\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(y\mid x)]\) 2
Baseline \(\mathbb{E}[b(x)\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta]=0\), \(\hat A=R-b\) 3
GRPO \(\hat A_i=(R_i-\operatorname{mean}R)/(\operatorname{std}R+\epsilon)\) 4
RLOO \(\hat A_i=R_i-\frac1{G-1}\sum_{j\ne i}R_j=\frac{G}{G-1}(R_i-\operatorname{mean}R)\) 4
PPO clip \(\min\big(\rho\hat A,\ \operatorname{clip}(\rho,1\pm\varepsilon)\hat A\big)\), \(\rho=\pi_\theta/\pi_{\mathrm{old}}\) 5
KL estimator \(k_3=r-1-\log r\), \(r=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}/\pi_\theta\) 5
KL optimum \(\pi^\star\propto\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\,e^{R/\beta}\); odds\(^\star\) = odds \(\cdot\,e^{1/\beta}\) 6
pass@\(k\) \(1-(1-p)^k\); \(1-\binom{n-c}{k}/\binom nk\) 7
Learnability \(\operatorname{Var}R=p(1-p)\); informative group \(1-p^G-(1-p)^G\) 9
R-Zero reward \(\max(0,\ 1-2|\hat p-\tfrac12|-\lambda|C_k|/B)\) 10
Absolute Zero \(1-\bar r\) if \(0<\bar r<1\), else \(0\) 10
SOAR \(\mathrm{Perf}_H(\mathrm{Train}(\theta,D_\phi))-\mathrm{Perf}_H(\theta)\) 10
PAIRED regret \(U(\pi_{\mathrm{ant}})-U(\pi_{\mathrm{pro}})\) 10
Symmetric noise \(\mathbb{E}[\tilde R\,\nabla_\theta\log\pi]=(1-2\eta)\nabla_\theta J\) 11
Bilinear spiral \(u^2+v^2\) grows by \((1+\eta^2)\) per step 12
Fisher identity \(\nabla_\theta\log p(y^\star\mid x)=\mathbb{E}_{p(z\mid x,y^\star)}[\nabla_\theta\log p(z,y^\star\mid x)]\) 14
RL vs likelihood \(\nabla_\theta p=p\,\nabla_\theta\log p\) 14
Data processing \(I(T;S)\le I(T;X)\) for \(T\to X\to S\) 15
Verifier gap prec \(=\frac{p\mathrm{TPR}}{p\mathrm{TPR}+(1-p)\mathrm{FPR}}\); prec \(>p\iff\mathrm{TPR}>\mathrm{FPR}\) 16
Condorcet \(\sum_{k>n/2}\binom nk q^k(1-q)^{n-k}\); error \(\le e^{-2n(q-1/2)^2}\) 17.1
Plurality margin \(\Pr[w\text{ ties or beats }t]\le e^{-n(\pi_t-\pi_w)^2/2}\) 17.2
Shared-error cap \(\rho q+(1-\rho)P_{\mathrm{maj}}(n)\to\rho q+1-\rho\) 17.3
Effective votes \(n_{\mathrm{eff}}=n/(1+(n-1)r)\to1/r\) 17.3
Voting ceiling \(\Pr_x[\text{mode of }\pi(\cdot\mid x)\text{ is the truth}]\) 17.3
Indistinguishability \(O(x)\overset{d}{=}O(x'),\ t\ne t'\Rightarrow\Pr[L(x){=}t]+\Pr[L(x'){=}t']\le1\) 17.4
Correlation from difficulty \(r=\operatorname{Var}_x[q(x)]/\big(\bar q(1-\bar q)\big)\) 17.3
Override gain \(\Delta\text{acc}=ea-(1-e)b>0\iff a/b>(1-e)/e\) 17.7
Samples to find \(1-(1-p)^k\); \(k_{90\%}=\ln10/(-\ln(1-p))\approx2.3/p\) 18
Strict check prec \(\ge\tfrac12\iff\mathrm{FPR}\le\frac{p}{1-p}\mathrm{TPR}\) 18.1
Verified vote truth wins \(\iff\pi_t\,\mathrm{TPR}>\max_w\pi_w\,\mathrm{FPR}_w\) 18.2
Coverage law \(C\approx\exp(a\,k^{b})\),  \(b<0\) 18.3
Resampling ceiling accuracy \(\le\) prec (16), for any budget \(K\) 18.4
FOBAR \(\mathbb P\propto\mathbb P_F^{\,\alpha}\,\mathbb P_B^{\,1-\alpha}\),  \(\alpha=\tfrac12\) 18.6
Model collapse \(\mathbb{E}[\sigma_t^2]=((N-1)/N)^t\sigma_0^2\) 19
Weak-to-strong PGR \(=\frac{\text{w2s}-\text{weak}}{\text{ceiling}-\text{weak}}\) 20
Standard error \(\sqrt{p(1-p)/N}\) 21
