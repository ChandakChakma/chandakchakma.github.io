Hi, I’m Chandak. These are my study notes on self-play and reinforcement learning for LLM reasoning: the definitions, derivations and worked examples I needed, each with exercises and answers. The series Foundations of Self-Play for LLM Reasoning is also available as one PDF.

The RL Engine Behind LLM Reasoning

Every method for training LLM reasoners, from PPO to GRPO to self-play, estimates the same policy gradient. This post derives it, shows why baselines add no bias, compares GRPO with RLOO, and explains what PPO clipping and the KL term really do.
