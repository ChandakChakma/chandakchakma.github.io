Model Collapse and Weak-to-Strong Generalization

Date: November 6, 2026  |  Estimated Reading Time: 3 min  |  Author: Chandak Chakma

Training only on your own outputs loses the tails; labels weaker than the student can still teach it. Two results that frame how far self-labels can go.

19 Model collapse

Key resultrecursive training loses the tails

Fit a Gaussian by maximum likelihood to \(N\) samples drawn from the previous generation’s fit. Since \(\mathbb{E}[\hat\sigma^2]=\frac{N-1}{N}\sigma^2\) for the maximum-likelihood variance, iterating gives \[\mathbb{E}[\sigma_t^2]=\Big(\frac{N-1}{N}\Big)^{t}\sigma_0^2\ \longrightarrow\ 0 .\]

Examplehow fast

\(N=100\), \(t=100\): \((0.99)^{100}=0.366\). Two thirds of the variance is gone, and the rare cases (the tails) go first.

Shumailov et al. (2024) show the same tail loss for language models trained recursively on their own outputs. Gerstgrasser et al. (2024) show that accumulating real and synthetic data, instead of replacing old data, keeps the error bounded.

Why it matters

R-Zero’s Solver trains only on labels it made itself: the pure “replace” regime. Keeping earlier data, anchoring to the base model, and adding outside data are the known fixes.

20 Weak-to-strong generalization

Definitionperformance gap recovered (Burns et al., 2023)

\[\mathrm{PGR}=\frac{\mathrm{Perf}_{\text{weak}\to\text{strong}}-\mathrm{Perf}_{\text{weak}}}{\mathrm{Perf}_{\text{strong ceiling}}-\mathrm{Perf}_{\text{weak}}}.\]

Strong models trained on a weaker model’s labels often recover a substantial part of the gap to their ceiling: labels worse than the student can still teach it. This is the optimistic counterpart of model collapse.

Why it matters

Noisy self-labels can still teach, as long as their errors are not the ones the student already believes. Self-labels are risky because their errors are, by construction, the student’s own beliefs.

Exercises

Exercise 11. In the Gaussian collapse model with \(N=50\), what fraction of the variance remains after \(50\) generations?

Show answer

\((49/50)^{50}=e^{50\ln0.98}=0.364\).
